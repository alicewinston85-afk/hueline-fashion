HUELINE ASSETS
==============

Every image here is original, generated for Hueline. catalog.js lists them in ASSETS, so
the site only ever requests files that exist.

In use now
- hero-poster.jpg            First frame of the hero film (../hero-main.mp4/.webm, pink silk)
- hero-close-1/2.mp4/.webm   Hero close-up panes (orchid silk, hands lifting marigold silk)
- dye-yard.mp4/.webm         Film band above the Atelier (the original dye-yard walk)
- look-01.jpg ... look-06.jpg  Lookbook (cobalt, orchid, marigold, lagoon, poppy, indigo)
                               Also the main photos for products HL-T-03, HL-E-01, HL-K-03,
                               HL-S-04, HL-R-01, HL-D-03
- mood-tailoring.jpg         Arch + product HL-T-02 (cobalt suit on marigold wall)
- mood-street.jpg            Arch + product HL-S-01 (lagoon hoodie on blush wall)
- mood-evening.jpg           Arch + product HL-E-03 (orchid halter on teal wall)

- mood-knit.jpg, mood-resort.jpg, mood-denim.jpg   The other three arches
- dye-cobalt.jpg, dye-marigold.jpg, dye-orchid.jpg, dye-lagoon.jpg   Colour story still lifes
- atelier.jpg                                       Atelier section (block printing)

Optional slots (add the file, then add its name to ASSETS in catalog.js)
- product-hl-x-00.jpg (e.g. product-hl-k-01.jpg)    Studio shot for any product still on a stock photo

Until a slot is filled, that spot shows the product's own photo, or a stock photo, or a
block in the product's dye colour, in that order.

Prompts for every slot are in image-prompts.md. Portrait 3:4 at about 900 x 1200 px works best.
